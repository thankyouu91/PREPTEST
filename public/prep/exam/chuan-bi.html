Check your equipment

The real test checks your machine before the scored parts begin. Work through these five, then start. Once the test begins it cannot be paused.

Headphones Not checked

You should hear a voice clearly. Set the volume where it is comfortable.

Microphone Not checked

Say "I am ready to take the test" out loud. We check the microphone picks you up.

Background noise Not checked

Stay completely silent for five seconds. A noisy room makes the speaking parts harder to mark.

Connection Not checked

Your answers are saved as you go, so the test needs a steady connection.

Webcam Not used in practice

The real sitting takes your photograph and watches the room. Practice does not, so nothing here is recorded or sent anywhere.

Speaking Tips

It is important to speak naturally during the test.

Here are some tips:

  • Speak at a normal speed like you would during a conversation.
  • Speak like you are talking to a person on the phone. There is no need to speak too carefully or slowly.
  • Speak at a normal volume, not too loud or too soft.
